# Digital Human Portrait Frame

- Canvas: 720x1280, 30 fps.
- Avatar remains the primary subject and is never covered above the lower 28% of frame.
- Hook title occupies the top safe area with at most two lines.
- Captions occupy the lower safe area with at most two lines per cue.
- Local or licensed B-roll may appear in a bounded cutaway window.
- When media is absent, use restrained fact cards and typography on a neutral dark background.
- Cover is captured without platform UI or controls.
